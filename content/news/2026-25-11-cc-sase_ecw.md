Together with Tine Hanrieder, I'm co-chairing the 2027 [SASE-Digit Early Career Workshop](https://sase.org/events/2027-glasgow/#early-career-workshop). The SASE-Digit Early Career Workshop (ECW) is a one-day workshop that provides an opportunity for a longer and deeper discussion of applicants' conference papers. It takes place the day before the start of the annual conference (29 June 2027). The 2027 Early Career Workshop will be hosted at the University of Strathclyde, with senior SASE professors. The (hard) deadline for submissions: 16 December 2026.

A detailed call can be found [here](/2027_cfa_sase_ecw.pdf).
